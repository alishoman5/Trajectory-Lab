ATE plotting fix
================

- Fixed the GUI ATE-vs-time plot to show true 3D ATE magnitude:
    sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2)
  instead of plotting Trajectopy's signed X/Y/Z deviation components as separate lines.
- The plot now shows exactly one non-negative line per algorithm.
- Renamed the tab to "3D ATE vs Time" and the y-axis to "3D ATE magnitude [m]".
- Existing Trajectopy numeric metrics, native plots, speed diagnostics, segment heatmap,
  and dataset-history calculations are unchanged.
